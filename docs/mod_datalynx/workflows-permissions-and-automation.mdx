# Workflows, permissions, and automation

Datalynx becomes much easier to operate when permissions and moderation rules are explicit.

## Areas to define

- who can create, edit, approve, or delete entries
- whether users can edit after submission
- what moderation or review states are needed
- whether entries feed another administrative or reporting process

## Rollout recommendation

Start with a tightly scoped pilot, document the workflow that worked, and turn that into a reusable administrator template for later course teams.
